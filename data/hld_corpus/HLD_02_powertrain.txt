EngineManager connects to TransmissionManager.
TransmissionManager connects to DiagnosticManager.
DiagnosticManager connects to SensorManager.

6. Functional Flow

EngineManager provides TorqueRequest to TransmissionManager.
TransmissionManager provides GearState to DiagnosticManager.
