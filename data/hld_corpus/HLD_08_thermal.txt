DiagnosticManager uses interface DiagnosticInterface.

5. Communication and Dependencies
ThermalController connects to CoolantManager.
CoolantManager connects to FanManager.
FanManager connects to SensorManager.
SensorManager connects to DiagnosticManager.
DiagnosticManager depends on ThermalController.

6. Functional Flow
ThermalController provides CoolantTemperature to CoolantManager.
CoolantManager provides FanSpeed to FanManager.
FanManager provides ThermalState to SensorManager.
