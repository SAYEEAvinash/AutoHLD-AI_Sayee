MemoryManager uses interface MemoryInterface.
UserProfileManager uses interface UserProfileInterface.

5. Communication and Dependencies
SeatController connects to MotorManager.
MotorManager connects to MemoryManager.
MemoryManager connects to UserProfileManager.
UserProfileManager depends on SeatController.

6. Functional Flow
SeatController provides SeatPosition to MotorManager.
MotorManager provides MotorCurrent to MemoryManager.
MemoryManager provides ProfileId to UserProfileManager.
