4. Component Responsibilities

HealthManager uses interface HealthInterface.
DiagnosticManager uses interface DiagnosticInterface.

5. Communication and Dependencies
SafetyMonitor connects to WatchdogManager.
WatchdogManager connects to HealthManager.
HealthManager connects to DiagnosticManager.
DiagnosticManager depends on SafetyMonitor.

6. Functional Flow
SafetyMonitor provides HealthState to WatchdogManager.
WatchdogManager provides WatchdogStatus to HealthManager.
HealthManager provides DiagnosticRequest to DiagnosticManager.
